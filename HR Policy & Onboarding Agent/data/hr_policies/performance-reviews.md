# Performance Reviews and Promotions

## 1. Review Cycles
The company conducts formal performance reviews bi-annually, in June and December. These reviews include self-assessments, peer feedback, and manager evaluations. 

## 2. Promotions
Promotions are considered during the December review cycle. Eligibility for promotion requires at least one year of tenure in the current role and a performance rating of "Exceeds Expectations" or higher in the most recent review cycle. 

## 3. Performance Improvement Plans (PIP)
If an employee receives a "Needs Improvement" rating, they may be placed on a 30, 60, or 90-day Performance Improvement Plan (PIP). The PIP will outline specific, measurable goals. Failure to meet these goals within the specified timeframe may result in termination of employment.

## 4. Continuous Feedback
Managers are expected to hold weekly or bi-weekly 1:1 meetings with their direct reports to provide ongoing feedback, independent of the formal review cycles.
