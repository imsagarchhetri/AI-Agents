# Benefits Overview

## 1. Health Insurance
The company covers 90% of the medical, dental, and vision insurance premiums for employees, and 75% for dependents. Coverage begins on the first day of the month following your start date. 

## 2. Retirement (401k)
Employees are eligible to participate in the company's 401(k) plan immediately upon hire. The company matches 100% of the first 4% of employee contributions, and 50% of the next 2%. Company matches vest immediately.

## 3. Wellness Program
Employees receive a $100 monthly wellness stipend. This can be used for gym memberships, fitness classes, mental health applications, or ergonomic home office equipment. 

## 4. Professional Development
The company encourages continuous learning. Employees have an annual budget of $1,500 for professional development, which covers conferences, certifications, and online courses. Pre-approval from a direct manager is required.
