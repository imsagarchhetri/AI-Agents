# Code of Conduct

## 1. Professional Behavior
We expect all employees to foster a collaborative, inclusive, and respectful work environment. Discriminatory, harassing, or bullying behavior of any kind is strictly prohibited and will result in disciplinary action, up to and including termination.

## 2. Anti-Harassment
The company maintains a zero-tolerance policy towards sexual harassment, racial discrimination, and any other form of harassment. Any incidents should be immediately reported to the HR department or anonymously through the company's whistleblower hotline.

## 3. Conflict of Interest
Employees must avoid any situation where personal interests could conflict with their professional duties. Outside employment or freelance work that competes directly with the company's business must be disclosed and approved by HR.

## 4. Dress Code
We observe a "dress for your day" policy. While casual attire is generally acceptable, employees are expected to dress professionally when meeting with clients or external stakeholders.
