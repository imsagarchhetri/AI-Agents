## Policy Summary
All remote employees are eligible for a $1,200 annual home office stipend. Equipment must be approved by IT and HR. Laptops must meet minimum security specs (TPM 2.0, encrypted SSD). Stipends are reimbursed quarterly via payroll. Violations may result in disciplinary action.
