# Paid Time Off (PTO) and Leave Policy

## 1. Accrual and Eligibility
All full-time employees accrue 15 days of Paid Time Off (PTO) per year, accumulating at a rate of 1.25 days per month. Employees become eligible to use accrued PTO after completing their first 30 days of employment.

## 2. Sick Leave
Employees are granted 10 days of paid sick leave annually on January 1st. Sick leave does not carry over to the next calendar year. A doctor's note may be required for sick leave exceeding 3 consecutive business days.

## 3. Parental Leave
The company provides up to 12 weeks of fully paid parental leave for the birth, adoption, or foster placement of a child. This is available to all full-time employees who have been with the company for at least 6 months.

## 4. Bereavement Leave
Employees are entitled to 5 days of paid bereavement leave in the event of the death of an immediate family member.

## 5. Holidays
The company observes 10 standard national holidays. If a holiday falls on a weekend, it will be observed on the closest preceding Friday or following Monday.
