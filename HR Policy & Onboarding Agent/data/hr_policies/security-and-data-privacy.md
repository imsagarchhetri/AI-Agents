# Information Security and Data Privacy

## 1. Password Policy
All employee accounts must be secured with strong passwords (minimum 12 characters, including numbers and symbols). Passwords must be changed every 90 days. Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) is strictly required for all company systems.

## 2. Handling of Personal Identifiable Information (PII)
Employees handling customer or employee PII must strictly adhere to data privacy laws (e.g., GDPR, CCPA). PII must never be stored on unencrypted local drives or shared via unauthorized external channels.

## 3. Device Security
Company-issued laptops and mobile devices must remain encrypted using MDM (Mobile Device Management) software. If a company device is lost or stolen, it must be reported to the IT Helpdesk immediately to initiate a remote wipe.

## 4. Acceptable Use of Systems
Company networks and systems are strictly for business use. Downloading unauthorized software or accessing malicious websites is prohibited.
