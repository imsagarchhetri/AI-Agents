# Travel and Expense Reimbursement

## 1. Travel Booking
All business-related flights and hotels must be booked through the company's designated corporate travel portal (Concur). Flights under 6 hours must be booked in Economy class. Flights exceeding 6 hours may be booked in Premium Economy or Business Class with VP approval.

## 2. Meals and Incidentals
Employees traveling for business are provided a daily per-diem of $75 for meals and incidentals ($100 for high-cost cities like NYC or SF). Receipts are required for any single expense exceeding $25.

## 3. Expense Submission
Expense reports must be submitted via Concur within 30 days of the transaction. Late submissions may be rejected. Reimbursements are typically processed within two payroll cycles.

## 4. Non-Reimbursable Expenses
The following items will not be reimbursed by the company:
- Traffic citations or parking tickets
- Personal entertainment (movies, mini-bar)
- Upgrades for flights or hotels
