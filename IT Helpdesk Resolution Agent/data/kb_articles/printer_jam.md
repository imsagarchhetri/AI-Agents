## Steps to Resolve
1. Open the main paper tray and remove any crumpled or stuck paper.
2. Check the rear access door and gently pull out any jammed paper.
3. Reload the tray with fresh standard A4 paper.
4. Press the "Resume" or "OK" button on the printer console to clear the error.
5. If the error remains, try power cycling the printer.
