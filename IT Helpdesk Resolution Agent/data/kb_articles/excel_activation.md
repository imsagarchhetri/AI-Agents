## Steps to Resolve
1. Close all Office applications.
2. Open the Software Center or self-service portal.
3. Click "Renew Office 365 License" to force a sync.
4. Re-open Excel and sign in with the corporate email if prompted.
5. If the issue persists, contact the procurement team for a license review.
